# Batches 5–10: implementation and acceptance record

This change continues PR170. Automated verification is not a substitute for authenticated phone and provider acceptance. Do not mark every audit task closed just because deployment succeeds.

## Implemented in this release

- Booking recovery: account-scoped tab drafts expire after 24 hours, fresh quotes on reopening, separate pickup/shop and delivery access instructions, saved pending checkout links, checkout return/thank-you page and tracking code.
- Pricing: proportional road mileage and existing launch fares, separate surcharge breakdown, configurable shopping product/quantity/weight/dimension limits with customer acknowledgement, actor/time/before/after pricing history, configurable 20% commission and minimum earnings. New orders snapshot earnings; historical orders retain their previous rate. Explicit initial defaults are £4.49 minimum earnings, 10 units per product, 15 kg total and 60 cm per package. These are implementation defaults, not an assertion of client-approved business policy. Manchester radius remains the existing 0/unrestricted setting until admin selects coverage. Existing BFM budget cap remains unchanged.
- Direct card booking: Stripe Checkout replaces compulsory wallet use in both customer forms. Only a verified paid GBP session creates an order. Immutable server quotes/drafts, customer/request uniqueness, Stripe idempotency keys and atomic finalization prevent double booking. Manual promos and automatic discount campaigns reserve usage with the checkout; redemptions are recorded on payment. Customer shopping budget is charged separately from service revenue, with unused funds refunded.
- Refunds: pre-collection cancellation queues an original-card refund atomically; wallet triggers explicitly exclude card orders. Receipt refunds and administrator refunds share a capped, idempotent queue. API-success/database-failure recovery finds existing Stripe refunds before creating another. Terminal refund states do not regress. Daily authenticated reconciliation plus admin retry handles pending/action-required refunds and processing fees. Admin and customer payment screens expose refund states; admin totals separate shopping funds, service fees, processor costs and refunds.
- Company-funded purchasing: dedicated GBP Stripe Issuing card references only (no PAN/CVC storage), business balance/capacity checks before checkout, unique card assignment, admin verification while inactive, shelf-price confirmation, customer approval for higher prices/substitutions, frozen approved basket and exact basket spending control before activation. Receipt settlement reconciles captured purchases plus pending authorization amounts and disables the card. Drivers can report shop problems to admin; admin sees shopping duration and reports. A funded card cannot silently transfer to a different driver. The company must enable/fund Issuing and securely provide cards to drivers; this code does not create a financial account or pretend funding is configured.
- Driver integrity: database assignment checks approval, vehicle and paid status under locks; document/proof paths must exist in storage; OTP attempts, delivered state, proof and earnings settle in one database transaction. New driver offers/statements use the agreed earnings, GBP, distance/time and shopping item details. Historical synthetic earnings are no longer double-counted or falsely marked paid.
- Tracking: foreground GPS, explicit permission/offline/stale states, retry/reconnect recovery, cancellation-safe cleanup, no fabricated audio/video background guarantees. Polling/realtime responses reject older positions; route requests retain bounded frequency and stale-response protection. Background browser GPS remains best effort.

## Validation

15 automated regression groups: invalid shopping input, unauthorized reads/mutations, proportional pricing, service boundaries, GPS age/stage handling, booking/receipt rollback and replay, card payment mismatches and replay, cancellation/refund caps and terminal state, business purchase capacity, promo reservations, pricing history authorization, approved basket freeze, wrong OTP lockout, completion rollback on earnings failure, receipt/card reconciliation and legacy/new earnings snapshots. TypeScript, icon check and optimized production build passed before publishing. The migration was checked against the actual Supabase schema inside a rolled-back transaction; no test customers were charged.

## Release procedure

Apply `20261030211100_direct_checkout_and_shopping_operations.sql` using the connected Supabase migration API before deploying this application. Existing tables are extended; no replacement order/wallet tables are introduced. Remote migration timestamps are assigned by the connector and may differ from source filenames. Do not run CLI db push blindly.

Required Stripe webhook event coverage: `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `refund.created`, `refund.updated`, `refund.failed`. The authenticated daily reconciliation route also retrieves refund state directly, but webhook configuration still needs provider verification. `CRON_SECRET`, `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` were confirmed present as environment metadata, without reading their values.
